The opportunity · context 4 / 26
The tradition

Cross-project reviews worked.

For years Visma engineers did 1–2 cross-project code reviews per year. Outside eyes catch what inside eyes miss. People learn faster from peers than from courses.

What's different in 2026. Code is no longer the artifact that matters most — how an engineer works with AI is. A reviewer at a higher maturity stage carries the most concrete, transferable knowledge in the company. We should make that exposure routine.

The hypothesis. Pair every engineer once or twice a year with someone strictly above them on the AI maturity rank. Make the session about workflow, prompting, verification, orchestration — not architecture nitpicks. Capture short feedback. Let the org see what's spreading.

Positioning · what this app is 8 / 26
Scope

What this is, and what it isn't.

It is

  • A peer exposure tool — book sessions, capture what was learned
  • About best practices, prompting, verification, orchestration
  • A way to surface insights and themes across the org
  • A way to make existing AI champions discoverable
  • A coach's workspace for arranging high-leverage pairings

It isn't

  • The place where AI maturity levels get set — that's the coach 1×1 check-in
  • A replacement for team code review
  • A performance evaluation system
  • A Slack / approval workflow — that stays out-of-band
  • An LMS or training catalog

How it works · core loop 9 / 26
One cycle

Six-month cycle, four moves.

01 · COACH

Pick a reviewer one level above

App suggests the top 5 candidates by rank, project diversity, and load. Coach agrees the pairing in Slack/email, then records it.

›
02 · BOTH

Schedule a 1–1.5 hour session

MVP: a date is recorded. Phase 2: pick from free/busy slots and the meeting lands in both Google calendars.

03 · BOTH

The session: workflow, not code

The reviewee walks through how they actually work with AI. The reviewer shows what one rung up looks like.

›
04 · BOTH

Short feedback, both directions

Reviewer notes observed maturity + one habit to adopt. Reviewee notes top thing learned + what they'll try in two weeks.

Patterns · design principles 10 / 26
Ten guardrails

Patterns the app commits to.

Approval lives out-of-band
Coach agrees the pairing in Slack/email. The app records the agreed pairing — no in-app accept/decline ping-pong.
Levels are read, never set in the normal flow
Levels come from the Q1/Q4 check-in. The app reads them. A coach can manually record one if missing.
Strict-greater matching
Reviewer's rank on the 9-cell grid is strictly higher than the reviewee's. Exposure is the point.
One sidebar, not four apps
Engineers who coach see both nav areas. Role-mixed users get one workspace.
Anonymity boundary, labeled
BG / MD see aggregates. Raw feedback only on the reviewee's own session detail. Every screen labels its audience.
Stale data is flagged, not hidden
Unverified levels, old surveys, last-import date — all surfaced as nudge banners, never silent.
Magenta is reserved
Visma magenta `#E10078` only for the one primary action per screen. Level and status palettes never collide with it.
Auto-pair is a safety net
If a coach is silent for too long, the system pairs and notifies. Manual remains the norm; auto is the floor.
Email is the only system channel in MVP
No Slack bot. Confirmations, T-24h, T-3d, overdue, digest — all email. Quiet hours and rate caps honored.
Cross-project means cross-project
Reviewer and reviewee must be on different projects. Cross-company is allowed and slightly preferred.

Considerations · open questions 11 / 26
Things to land before launch

What we still need to decide.

GDPR & retention

Raw feedback is personal data. Proposal: retain 2 years raw + indefinite aggregates. Confirm with Visma DPO.

Coach coverage

~16% of employees in the master sheet have no coach assigned. Backfill before cycle starts or these engineers are stuck.

Brand verification

Magenta `#E10078` is our default. Need confirmation against Visma's brand kit before public rollout.

Cycle cadence

6-month cycles match "1–2 reviews/year". Quarterly is more pressure but more learning. Pick one.

Cross-company allowed?

Visma has several legal entities. We assume yes; HR sign-off needed for contract / IP boundaries.

Calendar scopes

Phase 2 needs Google Calendar OAuth scope. Workspace admin approval required.
